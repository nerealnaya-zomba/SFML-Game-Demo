# Enemy Sprite Sources

- `WraithBat` is based on `Bat 32x32` by MoikMellah from OpenGameArt: https://opengameart.org/content/bat-32x32
- `VoidSlime` is based on `Jumping Blob` by AntumDeluge from OpenGameArt, licensed CC0: https://opengameart.org/content/jumping-blob
- `DreadScorpion` is based on `Scorpy Scorp side scroller enemy` by madameberry from OpenGameArt, released to the public domain / CC0: https://opengameart.org/content/scorpy-scorp-side-scroller-enemy

The frames were cropped into the game's folder structure and then recolored in runtime to fit the dark-fantasy look.
